About Me

I am currently serving as a Research Fellow at Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore, and conduct research and projects within the SP Group–NTU Joint Lab, advised by Prof. Xu Yan.

My research focuses on data-driven control and operation of power systems, exploring innovative applications of machine learning, deep learning, reinforcement learning, generative AI, and large language models. Additionally, I have 5+ years of hands-on software development experience and extensive R&D project experience.
